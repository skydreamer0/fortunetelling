# Limited exact-claim/fact-set provenance (#27)

`describeFactProvenance` describes an owned FactBoundAnalysis from the existing
internal consumer. It is not finalized Signal evidenceGroup V2, a scoring input,
or a public export. Production packs still abstain. Issue #27 stays open.

## Exact duplicate key and retained branches

The canonical key contains the exact system, exact claimId, and canonically
sorted complete semantic FactIdentity values. Those include primary chart variant,
native natal period, adapter and calculation versions. Diagnostic fact/snapshot
IDs, source array pointers, school, school version and stance are not key fields.
No aliases or heuristics equate different claims or facts; natal alone never
groups the whole chart. Source identities are neither rewritten nor migrated.

Each group retains every exact school/version/stance branch, rule sources and
unknown verification state. Opposing branches are not reduced to a maximum or
vote. Groups retain full resolved facts and exact source references. The separate
`ifpg1` diagnostic digest is not authentication: distinct full keys with an equal
digest cause rejection rather than combination or ambiguous overlap references.

## Shared facts and trust

Distinct groups have pairwise overlap records with exact shared resolved facts.
Partial intersections do not union groups, including transitive overlap chains.
No overlap record only means no identical facts in this limited representation;
it does not establish independence. `independence: not-established` applies to
every descriptor, including disjoint groups. Unmodelled dependencies may remain.

The descriptor retains the original complete owned analysis, including snapshot,
FactStore, interpretation, inputs, assessments, comparisons and unavailable packs.
Only privately registered owned fact analyses are accepted. Current snapshot
environment and input/output references are rechecked; serialized lookalikes and
legacy analyses are rejected. Existing per-registry validation remains stricter
about registry origin and is unchanged. `validateFactProvenance(candidate,
expected)` compares complete bytes against a current owned descriptor and
rechecks its analysis. Matching short hashes do not establish validity. This is
private birth data, not anonymized data or a deserialization API.

## Explicit uncovered scope

Current conclusions have no domain or native trigger-event metadata. This slice
does not invent those fields or claim general trigger equivalence. Distinct
descriptions of transformations, meeting relations, stem relations or different
domains are not resolved into generalized source families. Semantic aliases,
Signal V2 migration, validationRef, complete EvidenceStore/export/MCP integration,
real multi-school rules and unsupported adapters remain open. #28/#29 must not
treat this limited grouping as finalized general provenance or independent votes.

No changes to ifa1/ia1/cs1/sn1/f1 identities, existing Signal IDs, Report 7,
ranking/scoring, forecasts, golden fixtures, UI or production rules are made.
Tests with varied evaluator outputs are synthetic contract probes only; the
retained synthetic catalog's production source and identity are unchanged.
